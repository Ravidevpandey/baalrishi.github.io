What Antarodaya means

Antar (within, the inner self) + Udaya (rising, dawn) = the rising within.

Just as the sun clears the darkness each morning, Antarodaya exists to help clarity, calm and confidence rise within you—through kundli, palm reading and mantra practice.

Our mark shows a sun rising from a lotus: the lotus for a pure inner self, the sun for the light of new understanding.
